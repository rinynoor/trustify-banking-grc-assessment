# Project Brief — v0.1

## Title
Indonesia Banking Cyber Resilience Assessment — November 2026

## Consulting identity
Trustify (fictional working brand)

## Engagement type
Independent real-world news-based case study, simulated financial institution; **not a real client engagement**.

## Problem statement
Public reports of increased cyber threat activity in Indonesia's financial sector motivate a structured assessment of how a hypothetical bank should govern, detect, respond to, and manage related risks. Source claims require independent verification before use as findings.

## Assessment questions
1. Which public claims are substantiated, and what do they actually establish?
2. What plausible banking cyber-risk scenarios follow from those threats?
3. What controls and governance practices would be relevant to a simulated bank?
4. Which regulations and frameworks are applicable, and with what evidence requirements?
5. What actions should management prioritize, based on stated assumptions?

## Deliverables
Evidence register; risk register; risk methodology; compliance/control matrix; privacy and third-party assessment; incident-response playbook; executive report; review log; KPI scorecard.

## Boundaries
No direct system access, vulnerability scanning, account data, real bank audit, breach attribution, or assertions of actual regulatory violations.

## Acceptance criteria (proposed targets, not achievements)
- 100% mandatory deliverables completed and reviewed
- >=95% material claim traceability to reviewed sources
- 100% high/critical simulated risks with documented treatment plan and owner
- >=90% in-scope control assessment coverage (including explicitly marked unknown/not assessed)
- >=90% reviewer findings closed with evidence of change

# Scope & Methodology — v0.1

**In scope:** public-source evidence review; hypothetical bank assets and workflows; governance, risk, control and legal applicability mapping; management recommendations.

**Out of scope:** testing or certifying real institutions, accessing production systems, obtaining customer data, or claiming successful attacks without evidence.

## Method
1. Define material claims and source selection criteria.
2. Record original source URL, publisher, publication date, access date, quote/paraphrase, and limitations.
3. Mark claims `pending`, `reported`, `corroborated`, or `contradicted`; corroboration requires genuinely independent sources.
4. Develop simulated risk scenarios and state assumptions.
5. Score likelihood and impact using documented criteria.
6. Map to controls and regulations, without inferring real-world compliance.
7. Review, revise, report, and calculate evidence-backed project KPIs.

# Trustify | Banking Cyber Resilience Assessment 2026

**Independent GRC portfolio case study** · **Status: In progress**

Trustify is a **proposed fictional consulting brand** for this portfolio. This project evaluates cybersecurity governance, risk, and compliance using publicly available reporting on cyber threats affecting Indonesia's financial sector in November 2026, and a **fully simulated banking environment**.

> **Not a commissioned engagement.** No bank has engaged or endorsed this project. No real bank's security posture, controls, incidents, or compliance status are assessed or certified.

## Objective
Produce a defensible, traceable GRC assessment, clearly distinguishing verified public reporting from assumptions and simulated controls.

## Scope
- Threat intelligence and source verification
- Governance and accountability
- Cyber risk assessment and treatment
- Regulatory/control mapping (NIST CSF 2.0; ISO/IEC 27001:2022; relevant OJK rules and Indonesia's PDP law, subject to applicability review)
- Privacy, vendor risk, incident response, executive reporting

## How to work
1. Read `DISCLAIMER.md` and `01-project-charter/project-brief.md`.
2. Populate `02-threat-intelligence/evidence-register.csv` with actual URLs, publication dates, and precise claims. **Unverified entries must remain pending.**
3. Link each analytical assertion to evidence IDs; distinguish threat signals from confirmed incidents.
4. Populate risk, compliance, and treatment records. Do not invent observed controls or incidents.
5. Review each artifact and record revisions in `09-quality-assurance/`.
6. Calculate KPI results only from reviewed records. Targets are not actual achievements.

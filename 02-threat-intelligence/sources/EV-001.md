## Evidence Statement

FortiGuard Labs dashboard data reported on
9 November 2026 at approximately 20:00 WIB
indicated around 1.2 million detected cyberattack
activities targeting Indonesia's financial and
banking sectors during the preceding 24 hours.

## Verification and Limitations

- The figure is based on vendor threat telemetry.
- It does not represent confirmed successful breaches.
- No specific bank compromise is established by
  this evidence.
- The dashboard observation is time-dependent.

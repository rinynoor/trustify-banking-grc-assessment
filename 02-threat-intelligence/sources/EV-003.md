## Evidence Statement

BSSN clarified that cyberattack activity detected
by FortiGuard Labs does not necessarily represent
confirmed cybersecurity incidents in Indonesia's
financial sector.

## Supporting Information

- BSSN strengthened financial-sector incident readiness.
- Incident response coordination was emphasized.
- Detected attack activity must be distinguished
  from confirmed successful compromises.

## GRC Interpretation

Threat telemetry is useful for risk identification,
but is insufficient on its own to prove a security
breach, financial loss, or regulatory violation.

## Evidence Limitations

- No direct access to bank security logs.
- No independent forensic verification.
- No specific bank compromise established.

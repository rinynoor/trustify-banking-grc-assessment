## Evidence Statement

OJK called for increased cybersecurity vigilance
among Indonesian banks following reports of
heightened cyber threat activity in November 2026.

## GRC Interpretation

The regulatory response supports assessing:

1. Cybersecurity governance and oversight
2. Threat monitoring and escalation
3. Incident response readiness
4. Operational resilience
5. Transaction anomaly monitoring

## Verification Limitations

- This is evidence of a reported regulatory response.
- It does not establish that any specific bank
  experienced a successful cyberattack.
- It does not prove a regulatory violation.
- The source must be checked against the original
  article before final validation.

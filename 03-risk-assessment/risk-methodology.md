# Simulated Risk Scoring Methodology

Risk score = likelihood (1–5) × impact (1–5). This is an **illustrative internal model**, not a bank's actual approved risk methodology.

- Likelihood 1: Rare; 2: Unlikely; 3: Possible; 4: Likely; 5: Almost certain (all require scenario-specific justification).
- Impact 1: Negligible; 2: Minor; 3: Moderate; 4: Major; 5: Severe.
- Proposed bands: Low 1–5; Medium 6–12; High 13–19; Critical 20–25.
- Inherent score is before controls. Residual score **must remain blank** until controls and effectiveness have been assessed, even in simulation.
- Do not interpret simulated scores as findings about real banks.

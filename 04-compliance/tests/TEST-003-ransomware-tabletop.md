# TEST-003 — Ransomware Recovery Tabletop

## Assessment Scope
Project: Trustify Banking Cyber GRC Assessment
Risk ID: CR-002
Control ID: CTRL-002
Test ID: TEST-003
Assessment Type: Simulated tabletop exercise
Status: NOT TESTED

## Scenario
At 09:00, a fictional bank's mobile banking service
becomes unavailable. The SOC identifies indicators
consistent with ransomware activity.

This is a fictional exercise, not a real banking incident.

## Exercise Objectives
1. Validate incident escalation decisions.
2. Evaluate isolation and containment planning.
3. Evaluate backup restoration planning.
4. Assess RTO and RPO against exercise targets.
5. Document recovery authorization and evidence.

## Exercise Targets
- RTO: 4 hours
- RPO: 15 minutes
- Backup: isolated and integrity-verified
- Recovery: authorized before production restoration

## Required Decisions
- Confirm incident commander.
- Identify affected services and dependencies.
- Determine containment actions.
- Select verified recovery point.
- Approve restoration sequence.
- Validate service integrity before reopening.

## Evidence Required
- Incident timeline
- Decision log
- Recovery plan
- RTO/RPO calculation
- Recovery approval record

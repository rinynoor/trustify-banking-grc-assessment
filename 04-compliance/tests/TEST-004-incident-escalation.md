# TRUSTIFY — TEST-004 Incident Escalation Tabletop

## Assessment Information
- Test ID: TEST-004
- Related Control: CTRL-003
- Related Risk: CR-003
- Exercise Type: Simulated Tabletop
- Status: PENDING

## Scenario
A fictional digital banking monitoring system
detects multiple suspicious transactions
associated with a potentially compromised account.

The SOC must assess the alerts and determine
whether incident escalation is required.

No real customer data or banking systems are used.

## Exercise Objectives
1. Identify suspicious transaction indicators.
2. Classify incident severity.
3. Define escalation responsibilities.
4. Evaluate notification timelines.
5. Document incident response decisions.
6. Identify evidence gaps.

## Illustrative Escalation Targets
- Alert triage: within 10 minutes
- SOC escalation: within 15 minutes
- Incident Commander notification: within 30 minutes
- Initial response decision: within 45 minutes

These are exercise assumptions,
not verified regulatory requirements.

## Required Evidence
- Synthetic transaction alerts
- Incident escalation timeline
- Escalation decision log
- Role and responsibility matrix
- Escalation performance assessment

## Limitations
This is a fictional cybersecurity GRC exercise.
It does not represent a confirmed incident
or an assessment of an actual bank.

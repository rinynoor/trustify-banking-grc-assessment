# TRUSTIFY — FIND-002 Recovery Governance Evidence Gap

## Finding Information
- Finding ID: FIND-002
- Assessment ID: TEST-003
- Related Control: CTRL-002
- Related Risk: CR-002
- Assessment Type: Simulated Ransomware Recovery Tabletop
- Finding Status: OPEN
- Finding Classification: Evidence Verification Gap

## Observation
The simulated recovery scenario contains six required
assessment artifacts.

The calculated scenario recovery metrics meet the
defined RTO and RPO targets.

However:
- Five decision records remain NOT_VERIFIED.
- Two recovery approval records remain NOT_VERIFIED.
- Backup integrity has not been technically demonstrated.
- No actual recovery exercise has been performed.

## Risk Implication
Without verified recovery decisions and approvals,
an organization may be unable to demonstrate that
restoration activities follow authorized procedures.

The simulated RTO/RPO results do not establish
production recovery capability.

## Recommended Corrective Actions
1. Conduct a documented tabletop walkthrough.
2. Record participant roles and exercise decisions.
3. Verify that recovery authorization procedures
   are defined and followed during the exercise.
4. Obtain evidence of backup integrity testing.
5. Document service validation and reopening criteria.
6. Perform a controlled technical recovery test
   where authorized and feasible.

## Closure Criteria
- Tabletop exercise evidence is reviewed.
- Required decisions and approvals are documented.
- Backup validation evidence is available.
- Recovery test results are evaluated.
- Assessment limitations are explicitly recorded.

## Current Conclusion
OPEN — Additional verification is required.

This finding concerns a fictional assessment.
It is not a finding against an actual bank.
